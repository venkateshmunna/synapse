// ARCHITECTURAL SPECIFICATION & RESEARCH FOUNDATION
Why Multi-Agent Debate Eliminates Hallucination

SYNAPSE operationalizes dialectical argumentation to transform LLM inference from brittle single-model monologues into error-correcting scientific discourse.

🧠 The Problem: The Single-Model Monologue

⌄

When querying an isolated language model, users face three unavoidable failure modes:

  • Autoregressive Cascades: Once an LLM samples a subtly erroneous token, its subsequent attention layers accept that assertion as ground truth, compounding the error across the entire answer.
  • Sycophancy & Anchoring: Models naturally conform to the user's premise. If asked "Why is strategy X bad?", a single model validates the negative framing rather than stress-testing alternatives.
  • Verification Blindspots: Prompting an LLM to "check its own work" fails because the model verifies with the same latent-space biases that caused the original error.

🔬 The Peer-Reviewed Research Foundation

⌄
Du et al., 2023 (MIT / Google Brain)

Improving Factuality and Reasoning via Multi-Agent Debate

Demonstrated that independent LLM agents cross-examining each other in structured rounds produce statistically significant drops in hallucinations and arithmetic errors compared to Chain-of-Thought or Self-Reflection prompting.

Chan et al., 2023 (Tsinghua / ChatEval)

Multi-Agent Refereeing and Evaluation (ChatEval)

Showed that multi-agent debate structures mirror human panel reviews, outperforming traditional single-judge LLMs in objectivity and nuance.

⚙️ The SYNAPSE 3-Tier Pipeline

⌄
Tier 1 — Isolated Exploration: Three heterogeneous models generate answers without seeing the others. This enforces diversity of thought and isolates baseline assertions.
Tier 2 — Cross-Critique (Adversarial Rebuttal): Each model receives the competitors' arguments. Agents must actively falsify opponent logic and defend or refine their claims against empirical counter-evidence.
Tier 3 — Impartial Synthesis: A 120B parameter judge model scores all rounds across 4 dimensions (factual validity, clarity, depth, originality) and produces a final, attributed synthesis combining only the verified arguments.

🛡️ Zero-Data-Leak Local Privacy Architecture

⌄

SYNAPSE features dual-engine routing: choose Groq Cloud for sub-second responses or switch to Ollama Local Mode. In Local Mode, prompts are dispatched strictly to localhost:11434 and stored in your browser's sandboxed localStorage. Zero telemetry or prompt tokens leave your device.
